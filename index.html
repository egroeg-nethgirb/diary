            Offline use

            Keeps an encrypted copy of your notebook on this device, so you can open it and write without internet. Anything you add offline uploads by itself when you are back online.

            Your token and password are never saved. Only an encrypted copy of your notes can stay on this device, and you can turn that off above. The app logs out after 15 minutes of inactivity.
